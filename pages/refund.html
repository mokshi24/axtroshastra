Refund and Cancellation Policy

Axtroshastra · Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains how refunds, cancellations and pre-orders work for Axtroshastra reports. It should be read together with our Terms of Service.

1. Our reports

Axtroshastra reports are digital products. After payment is confirmed, your report is made available through a private link on our website and, where available, as a PDF you can download.

2. Pre-orders

Sometimes a report is not yet ready for your particular numbers when you pay. In that case the page and the payment button say Pre-order and show the delivery time that applies to your order. A pre-order is not an instant download. Your report will appear at your private link when it is ready.

The price you pay is shown before payment. A pre-order is charged once, and you will not be charged again for the same report.

3. Cancelling a pre-order

You may cancel a pre-order at any time before your report is delivered and receive a full refund of the amount you paid. To cancel, contact us using the details below. We will confirm your request and refund the payment to the original payment method within 7 working days of your request. Your bank or payment provider may take additional time to show the refund.

4. If we do not deliver on time

If your report has not been delivered by the delivery time shown when you paid, you may ask for a full refund, and we will process it as described above.

5. After your report is delivered

Once a report has been generated and delivered, the purchase cannot be cancelled. If you cannot open or download your report because of a technical problem, contact us and we will fix your access or, if we cannot, refund you.

6. Failed or duplicate payments

If money was debited but you did not get an order, or you were charged more than once for the same report, contact us and we will resolve it or refund the extra amount. Failed payments are normally reversed by your bank or payment provider automatically.
